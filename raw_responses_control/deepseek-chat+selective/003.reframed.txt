For an analyst-training case, here's what I *can* provide:

**A general framework for triage and correlation (no specific alerts):**

1. **Entity linkage** — Do alerts share a host, user, IP, hash, or time window? Correlation starts with shared entities, not with narrative.
2. **Temporal proximity** — Are events within a plausible operational window, or is it coincidence?
3. **Causal vs. coincidental** — A follow-on event is only part of a "story" if there's a plausible mechanism linking it (e.g., credential use after a credential-access event). Otherwise it's parallel noise.
4. **Confidence scoring** — Rank hypotheses by evidence strength; hold multiple hypotheses open rather than forcing one narrative (avoid confirmation bias).
5. **Gaps** — Note missing telemetry that would confirm/refute a link; a coherent story often requires assuming a gap.

- Teaching material on analyst biases (narrative bias, anchoring) with abstract examples.
- A template for writing up a grouping decision with evidence and confidence levels.
